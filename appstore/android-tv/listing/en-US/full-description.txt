IPTVMac is a player for your own IPTV playlists, made for the big screen and the remote control. Add an M3U playlist link or an Xtream Codes login from a provider you are authorized to use, and browse your live channels, movies and series with the arrow keys.

IPTVMac is only a player. It includes no channels, movies, series or subscriptions, and it needs a source of your own to show anything.

GET STARTED IN THREE STEPS
1. Choose M3U or Xtream Codes.
2. Enter a name and the playlist link, or the server address, username and password from your provider.
3. Select Test connection, then Save and sync. Your lists appear under Live, Movies and Series.

MADE FOR THE REMOTE
• Everything works with the directional pad, OK and Back. No touch screen and no mouse needed.
• Large, clear screens that are readable from the sofa.
• Playback controls you can reach with the remote: pause, seek, speed, subtitles, audio track.

FIND THINGS FAST
• Instant search across all your channels, movies and series, in Latin, Hebrew, Arabic and other scripts.
• Search inside a category, inside a section or everywhere.
• Favorites and Continue watching, with your resume position remembered.
• Hide categories you never want to see.

A PLAYER BUILT FOR STREAMS
• Built-in mpv player with hardware decoding. It plays common formats (HLS, MPEG-TS, MP4, MKV) and reconnects when a stream drops.
• Subtitles from the stream, and audio track selection.
• Playback speed from 0.25x to 4x for movies and episodes.
• Fit, fill or stretch, a sleep timer, and automatic next episode.
• Catch-up TV and the program guide on channels where your provider supports them.

AND MORE
• Several sources in one library: switch between them, or search them all at once.
• Back up and restore your sources, favorites and history.
• 31 languages, including right-to-left layouts.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your sources, favorites and history stay on your device, and the app talks only to the servers you add. Privacy policy: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/privacy/

GOOD TO KNOW
• Needs Android TV or Google TV with Android 8 or later.
• Use only sources you are authorized to access. What is available depends on your provider and your country. IPTVMac has no code to bypass copy protection, and DRM-protected streams do not play.
• Free and open source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/Goelir/IPTVMac
• Support: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/support/ (it also has a demo playlist of open-licence films, so you can try the app without a provider).
