IPTVMac vsIPTV Smarters Pro

Both play Xtream Codes and M3U sources on many devices. The differences are in price, ads, tracking and who can read the code.

In short

Where IPTVMac is different

  • Nothing to buy, no ads and no tracking on any platform.
  • Open source (GPL-3.0); a native Mac app, not an iPad app running on the Mac.
  • Downloads for offline viewing on Mac, Windows and Android.

Where it is stronger: IPTV Smarters Pro

  • More devices: Apple TV and LG and Samsung smart TVs.
  • Its own extras: multi-view, parental controls and external EPG sources (some in its premium version).

IPTVMac is a media player. It does not include or host any content; use only sources you are authorized to access. IPTVMac is not affiliated with iptv-org.

Other IPTV players. IPTVMac runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, Android and Android TV. If you need Linux or something different: IPTVnator is free and open source and also runs on Windows and Linux. VLC can open an M3U playlist directly. IPTV Smarters Pro has a desktop app for macOS.
