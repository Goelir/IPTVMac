IPTVMac vsTiviMate

TiviMate is a polished player for Android TV. IPTVMac is free everywhere and also runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad and phones.

In short

Where IPTVMac is different

  • Free with nothing to buy; no account and no analytics.
  • Runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad and Android phones, not only on TVs.
  • Open source (GPL-3.0).

Where it is stronger: TiviMate

  • A full TV guide grid, recording, multi-view and Stalker portals.
  • Built for Android TV first, with years of polish there.

Other IPTV players. IPTVMac runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, Android and Android TV. If you need Linux or something different: IPTVnator is free and open source and also runs on Windows and Linux. VLC can open an M3U playlist directly. IPTV Smarters Pro has a desktop app for macOS.
