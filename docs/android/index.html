IPTV on Android,in your pocket.

A touch player with gestures, sound that keeps playing with the screen off, Picture in Picture and downloads for offline viewing. One free APK, no account.

Install

Install

Android 8 or later.

  1. Download the file on the phone and open it.
  2. Android asks to allow installs from your browser or Files app: allow it. If Play Protect warns about an app from outside the Play Store, choose Install anyway.
  3. Add your source. A banner tells you when a new version is out: tap Update now and the app downloads and installs it (Android asks you to confirm), and your data stays.

Not on the Play Store yet, so the install is a direct file. It is signed with the project's own key.

What you get

  • A player made for your thumbs.

    Double-tap to skip, swipe for volume and brightness, hold for 2x. Subtitles, audio tracks and a sleep timer are one tap away.

  • Keeps playing.

    Sound goes on with the screen off, with controls in the notification, and Picture in Picture keeps the picture while you use other apps.

  • Series, season by season.

    Continue watching at the right episode and minute, skip the intro and the credits, and download episodes for later.

Questions

Why is it not on the Play Store?

It is installed from a direct file for now, signed with the project's own key. Android asks you to allow the install once.

Which file do I need?

The arm64-v8a file suits almost every phone and tablet made since 2017. The download page lists the others.

How does it update?

A banner tells you when a new version is out. Tap Update now: the app downloads and installs it, and your data stays.
