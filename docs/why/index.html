Why IPTVMac.

Seven reasons, each with the place where you can check it yourself. And, just as plainly, what it does not do yet.

Download for Mac

Version 0.9.9 · 16 MB · Apple Silicon, macOS 14+

  1. 20:00

    Free. Really.

    There is nothing to buy: no subscription, no premium tier, no in-app purchases, no ads. You do not create an account with us; you only add the login your provider gave you. The code is open source under the GPL-3.0, so anyone can read it and build it.

    How to check: The license in the repository

  2. 20:30

    Nothing phones home.

    There are no analytics and no tracking libraries. The app talks to the servers you add and to GitHub, to check for updates. Passwords are stored on your device, never sent anywhere else.

    How to check: The privacy policy

  3. 21:00

    Found as you type.

    Your library lives in a local SQLite database with full-text search. Our automated tests fill it with 100,000 titles and check that each search returns in under a quarter of a second.

    How to check: The search speed test

  4. 21:30

    Plays the streams others drop.

    Playback runs on mpv, the engine behind many video players. It plays broken TS and HLS streams, reconnects by itself when a live channel drops, and decodes video in hardware.

    How to check: The live reconnect logic

  5. 22:00

    Made for each screen.

    Swift and SwiftUI on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Flutter with a Kotlin layer on Android and Android TV, and with a small C++ shell on Windows. No Electron and no web view, so it starts fast and stays light.

  6. 22:30

    Everything a viewer needs.

    Continue watching at the right episode and minute, skip intro and credits, Picture in Picture and several playlists in one library. On the Mac also catch-up on channels whose provider supports it; on Mac, Windows and Android also downloads for offline viewing.

    How to check: The full feature list

  7. 23:00

    In your language.

    31 languages, each in its own script, including Hebrew, Arabic, Persian and Urdu from right to left.

    How to check: The 31 translations

What it does not do (yet)

  • It does not import XMLTV guide files: the program guide comes from Xtream Codes logins.
  • On the Mac it needs Apple Silicon (M1 or later); Intel Macs are not supported. A local .m3u file cannot be opened yet, only a playlist link.
  • The Windows app is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so SmartScreen asks once before it runs.
  • The Android and TV apps are not on the Play Store yet; the TV app updates by installing the new file.
  • Downloads are for movies and episodes that are direct files; live channels cannot be recorded.
  • There is no Apple TV app.

IPTVMac is a media player. It does not include or host any content; use only sources you are authorized to access. IPTVMac is not affiliated with iptv-org.

Other IPTV players. IPTVMac runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, Android and Android TV. If you need Linux or something different: IPTVnator is free and open source and also runs on Windows and Linux. VLC can open an M3U playlist directly. IPTV Smarters Pro has a desktop app for macOS.
