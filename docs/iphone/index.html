Install

Install

iOS 17 or later.

  1. Open the App Store page and tap Get.
  2. Open IPTVMac and add your Xtream Codes login or M3U link.
  3. That is all: the App Store keeps it up to date.

Completely free: no ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account. 31 languages. Needs iOS 17 or later. The source is also here if you want to build it yourself.

What you get

  • Movies and series as posters.

    Browse the whole library by category, or search it as you type.

  • Live, with what is on now.

    Each channel shows the program that is on now, where your provider sends a guide.

  • Pick up where you stopped.

    Continue watching keeps the episode and the minute. Picture in Picture keeps playing while you use other apps.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.

Is the App Store version different from the one on GitHub?

It is the same app and the same code. The App Store version installs with one tap and updates itself after Apple has reviewed each version.

IPTVMac is a media player. It does not include or host any content; use only sources you are authorized to access. IPTVMac is not affiliated with iptv-org.

Other IPTV players. IPTVMac runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, Android and Android TV. If you need Linux or something different: IPTVnator is free and open source and also runs on Windows and Linux. VLC can open an M3U playlist directly. IPTV Smarters Pro has a desktop app for macOS.
