How to watch past programs (catch-up) on a Mac

Play a live channel that supports catch-up, click Watch from the past in the player, and pick a program from the guide. Your provider must keep an archive for that channel.

What catch-up is

Some providers record their channels for a few days. A player can then ask for a program that already aired. In IPTVMac this works with Xtream Codes logins on the Mac.

Which channels have it

Channels with an archive show a small clock icon in the channel list. How far back you can go depends on your provider.

Watch a past program

  1. Play the channel in IPTVMac.
  2. Click the clock button, Watch from the past, in the player controls.
  3. Pick a program from the list. If the channel has no program guide, pick a start time and a length by hand.
  4. The Live button takes you back to the channel's current broadcast.
