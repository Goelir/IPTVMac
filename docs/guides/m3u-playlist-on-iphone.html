How to play an M3U playlist on iPhone and iPad

Install IPTVMac from the App Store, choose M3U, give the source a name and paste the playlist link. The app sorts it into Live, Movies and Series.

What you need

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, and the link (URL) of an M3U or M3U8 playlist from a source you are allowed to use.

Add the playlist

  1. Get IPTVMac from the App Store. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
  2. Open it, choose M3U, type a name and paste the playlist link.
  3. Tap Test connection, then Save and sync.

How the entries are sorted

An M3U file does not say whether an entry is a channel, a movie or a series, so IPTVMac decides from the link and the group name, and treats files such as .mp4 or .mkv as movies. Everything else is live. The group-title of an entry becomes its category.

Tips

  • Search finds titles as you type, inside a category, a section or everywhere.
  • Star channels to keep them under Favorites; continue watching remembers the episode and the minute.
