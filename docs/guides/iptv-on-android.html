How to set up IPTV on an Android phone

Download the arm64-v8a APK, open it and allow the install, then add your Xtream Codes login or M3U link in IPTVMac.

Which file

The arm64-v8a APK suits almost every phone and tablet made since 2017. The Android page links the others.

Install

  1. Download the APK on the phone and open it.
  2. Android asks to allow installs from your browser or Files app: allow it. If Play Protect warns about an app from outside the Play Store, choose Install anyway.
  3. Open IPTVMac, choose Xtream Codes or M3U, fill in your details, then Test connection and Save and sync.

In the player

  • Double-tap to skip, swipe for volume and brightness, hold for 2x.
  • Sound keeps playing with the screen off, and Picture in Picture keeps the picture while you use other apps.

Updates

A banner tells you when a new version is out. Tap Update now; your data stays.

IPTVMac is a media player. It does not include or host any content; use only sources you are authorized to access. IPTVMac is not affiliated with iptv-org.

Other IPTV players. IPTVMac runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, Android and Android TV. If you need Linux or something different: IPTVnator is free and open source and also runs on Windows and Linux. VLC can open an M3U playlist directly. IPTV Smarters Pro has a desktop app for macOS.
